TTS Fine-Tuning Research — Marathi & Hindi Storyteller

From scraped audio to a copyright-safe synthetic pipeline · state as of 2026-07-05

Model: mar-hin-betacraft-tts Baseline: v6 Teacher/ceiling: Gemini-Sulafat

1. Objective

Build a single fine-tuned Indic Parler-TTS model that narrates children's stories in two voices — Sunita (Marathi) and Divya (Hindi) — in a warm, animated storytelling style, suitable for a commercial kids-content app.

2. The pivot — why synthetic data

Earlier models (v1–v6) trained on scraped / YouTube narration. Deep research confirmed that carries copyright risk for a commercial product. We therefore pivoted to a copyright-safe synthetic pipeline:

  • Gemini generates original children's stories (Devanagari, numbers spelled out).
  • Story text is synthesized to audio with Gemini-TTS Pro (voice Sulafat) — clean (wav, text) pairs with perfect alignment (the transcript IS the input text).
  • We fine-tune the Apache-2.0 Parler model on these pairs. Expressiveness transfers through the audio; a simple name-anchored caption picks the voice.
Licensing caveat: Gemini's ToS forbids training a competing TTS model on its output, so mar-hin-betacraft-tts is treated as research / PoC until the ToS is cleared or narration is commissioned. The base model (Indic-Parler, Apache-2.0) is unaffected.
