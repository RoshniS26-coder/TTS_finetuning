The air smelled like cold metal and fresh apples. Ankit stood in a giant white rocket ship, ready to fly all the way to the moon. His dad gave him a big hug and asked Ankit to promise to stay close to the ship once they landed. Then, the engines went roar, and they were off! When the ship landed with a soft thud, Ankit saw a group of friendly space-dogs playing tag on the grey moon dust. They looked so fun to play with, but the ship was far behind him. Will Ankit keep his promise to his dad to stay close to the ship?
