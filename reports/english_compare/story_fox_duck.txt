Once upon a time, a hungry fox lived near a quiet pond. Every day, a clever duck swam happily in the water, just out of the fox's reach. One evening, the fox called out sweetly, "Dear duck, why don't you come rest on the bank? I only wish to be your friend." The duck smiled but did not move an inch closer. "Friend," she said, "the water keeps me safe, and safe is where I will stay." The fox tried flattery, then tricks, then promises of grand feasts, but the duck simply paddled a little farther away each time. At last, tired and hungry, the fox gave up and slipped back into the forest. And the little duck kept swimming safely on her pond, proud that she had never been fooled.
